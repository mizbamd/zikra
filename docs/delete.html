Delete your account

App: Zikra — Android package com.mizbamd.zikra
Operator: Miz Mohammad · mizbauddin.md@gmail.com

You can delete your Zikra account and the data associated with it. Deletion removes the account, synced dhikr frames, and counts from Zikra’s servers. This cannot be undone.

In the app

If you are signed in, open the You tab, tap Delete account, and confirm.

That permanently deletes your account and associated synced data, wipes local app data on that device, and returns you to the welcome screen.

By email

Anyone can request deletion by emailing mizbauddin.md@gmail.com from the address on the account (or include that account email in the message) and asking us to delete the account and associated data.

Guest mode

Guest data stays on the device only. There is no account to delete. Uninstall the app, or clear app storage, to remove guest data on that device.
